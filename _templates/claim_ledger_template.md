## Tier Rules

- PROVED: formal proof, verified execution, paid result, signed contract, or directly reproducible artifact.
- EMPIRICAL: measured result with method, sample, and reproducibility caveats.
- OBSERVATION: interesting pattern without enough proof to carry the headline.
- SPECULATION: plausible but not established; never goes in title, abstract, pitch headline, or main investor claim.
- REMOVED: attractive claim deleted because evidence is weak or the risk is higher than the value.

## External Boundary

- Title/headline uses only PROVED claims.
- Abstract/summary uses PROVED and caveated EMPIRICAL claims.
- Main body separates proof from observations.
- Appendix can hold OBSERVATION claims if clearly labeled.
- SPECULATION is deleted or moved to private notes.
