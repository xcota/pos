# Personal OS

Your AI-powered second brain that knows you.

## Setup

1. Open this folder in terminal
2. Type `claude` and press Enter
3. The agent will guide you from there

That's it. No technical knowledge needed — just have a conversation.

## What happens

The agent will ask about 7 areas of your life (learning, health, people, wealth philosophy, rest, work, finances). Takes about 40-60 minutes. You can pause anytime and come back later.

After the conversation, it generates a personalized workspace that knows your context, goals, and patterns.

## Requirements

- [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code) installed
- A Claude subscription (Max or Pro)
