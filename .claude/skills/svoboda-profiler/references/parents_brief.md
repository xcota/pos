# Parents Brief — Origin Mapping

## Before asking

**IMPORTANT: This section touches family and childhood.** Before starting, tell the user:

> This next part is about your family — parents, childhood dynamics. Some of these questions can bring up difficult memories. You can skip any question, answer briefly, or skip this section entirely. There are no wrong answers, and refusing to answer is completely fine — it's information too.

Wait for their OK before proceeding. If they hesitate, remind them it's optional and you can move on.

## The Six Questions

Translate these to the user's language. The meaning matters, not the exact words.

1. **Mother.** What was she like when you were ~10? Not "good/bad" — what did she *do*? Controlling? Anxious? Present but absent? Too present?

2. **Father.** Same — what did he do? Present or background? Demanding? Punishing? Ignoring? Normal but distant?

3. **Between them.** What was the dynamic? Conflict, coldness, codependence, who dominated?

4. **Your role.** What was your role in the family system? Invisible? Mediator? Problem child? The one they projected onto?

5. **Separation.** When and how did you separate? Conflict? Quiet departure? Pushed out?

6. **Now.** Are you in contact? How? Anything unresolved?

## Interpretation Guide

Map answers to profile patterns:

| Parent pattern | → Possible profile pattern (verify, don't assume) |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Emotional overflow from parent → child | → May develop emotions-as-data processing style |
| Absent/avoidant parent | → May develop self-reliance as core identity |
| Controlling parent | → May develop system-rejection or system-mastery pattern |
| Unpredictable environment | → May develop control as personality structure |
| Parentification (child in adult role) | → May develop early maturity + hidden resentment |
| Enmeshment (blurred boundaries) | → Often correlates with late separation (>20) |
| Loss of parent early | → May develop reframing defense around irreversibility |

## Timing

Ask AFTER 5+ domains from Phase 1 are collected. The person needs to be warmed up and trust the process. Don't lead with family — it's the deepest block.

If they say "it's hard to describe" — offer to deduce from patterns already collected, present your hypothesis, let them correct. The correction is the real data.
