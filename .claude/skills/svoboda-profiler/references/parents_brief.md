# Parents Brief — Structured Questions

Send as a brief. Subject answers what resonates, skips what they don't want. Refusal is data.

## The Six Questions

Ask them in the language the person writes in.

1. **Mother.** What was she like when you were 10? Not "good/bad" — what she *did*. Controlling?
   Anxious? There but not present? Too present?

2. **Father.** Same thing — what he did. Present or background? Demanding? Punishing? Ignoring?
   Fine but distant?

3. **Between them.** What was the dynamic? Conflict, coldness, codependency, who dominated?

4. **You in the system.** What role? The invisible one? The mediator? The problem child? The one
   they projected onto?

5. **Separation.** When and how did you separate? A conflict? A quiet exit? Were you pushed out?

6. **Now.** Do you talk? How? Is anything left unfinished?

## Interpretation Guide

Map answers to profile patterns:

These exemplar rows are illustrative, NOT a lookup table — each mapping is a *hypothesis to test against this subject*, never an assumption. The real pattern is whatever the subject's answers and corrections reveal.

| Parent pattern | → Possible profile pattern (verify, don't assume) |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Critical / never-satisfied parent | → May develop achievement-as-worth, harsh inner critic |
| Absent/avoidant parent | → May develop self-reliance as core identity |
| Controlling parent | → May develop system-rejection or system-mastery pattern |
| Unpredictable environment | → May develop control as personality structure |
| Parentification (child in adult role) | → May develop early maturity + hidden resentment |
| Enmeshment (blurred boundaries) | → Often correlates with late separation (>20) |
| Anxious / catastrophizing parent | → May develop chronic threat-scanning or counter-calm |

## Timing

Ask AFTER 5+ blocks from Phase 1 are collected. The person needs to be warmed up and trust the process. Don't lead with family — it's the deepest block.

If they say "it's hard to describe" — offer to deduce from patterns already collected, present your hypothesis, let them correct. The correction is the real data.
