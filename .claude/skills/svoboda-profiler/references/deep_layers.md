# Deep Layers — Questioning Framework

## Layer 1: Эмоциональная гранулярность

**Core question:** Do they feel with high resolution, or is it "норм / не норм / перегрузка"?

**How to probe:**
- Don't ask "what do you feel?" — ask about a specific recent event and listen for emotional vocabulary.
- Count emotion words vs cognitive evaluations ("interesting", "logical", "makes sense" = cognitive; "joyful", "anxious", "tender" = emotional).
- Ask: "Right now, recording this — what's the feeling?" Present-moment access is the real test.

**What it reveals:**
- High granularity + high control = integrated emotional intelligence.
- Low granularity + claimed control = possible instrumentalization (emotions as data, not experience).
- High granularity + low control = emotional flooding risk.

**Key pattern:** If they describe emotions exclusively through outcomes ("I was productive" / "I lost weight") rather than states ("I felt restless") — that's instrumentalization.

## Layer 2: Мотивация инструмента

**Core question:** Why do they want what they say they want?

**How to probe:**
- Take their stated project/goal and ask: "What changes for you personally when this succeeds?"
- Look for circular answers ("I build it because I want to build it") — these mask the real driver.
- If they build systems to understand people/patterns → ask whether they do this intuitively or need external tools to bridge the gap.

**What it reveals:**
- Projection: building externally what's missing internally.
- Genuine curiosity vs compensatory mechanism.
- The gap between stated motivation and structural motivation.

## Layer 3: Проигрыши

**Core question:** Is there a loss they couldn't convert into a lesson?

**How to probe:**
- Don't ask "what did you lose?" — ask "what's the most expensive lesson you've paid for?"
- If every loss = lesson → ask: "Was there one where the price was too high for what you learned?"
- Listen for the ONE they circle back to, hedge around, or dismiss too quickly.

**What it reveals:**
- Pure loss-converters have no unprocessed wounds → either extraordinary resilience or emotional bypass.
- The loss they CAN'T convert reveals their actual attachment structure.
- Speed of reframing = speed of emotional bypass. Instant "it was a lesson" = didn't process the emotion.

## Layer 4: Одиночество

**Core question:** Do they know the difference between solitude (chosen) and loneliness (unchosen)?

**How to probe:**
- Ask directly: "Do you get lonely? Not 'want to be alone' — but lonely, wanting someone who isn't there?"
- Watch for hedging: "maybe, maybe not" = they feel it but can't classify it.
- If they immediately redirect to solutions ("I just stay busy") — they're managing it, not answering.

**What it reveals:**
- Loneliness that can't be classified = genuine unmet need that doesn't fit their self-model.
- Immediate solution-redirect = the need exists but is architecturally suppressed.
- Clear "no, never" = either true (rare) or dissociated from the feeling.

## Layer 5: Гнев

**Core question:** What triggers real anger — not annoyance, not disinterest, but ANGER?

**How to probe:**
- Ask for a specific recent example, not a category.
- If they say "I control it" → ask what it looks like BEFORE the control kicks in.
- Notice whether triggers are people-based or system-based.

**What it reveals:**
- System-based anger (broken process, ignored instructions) = identity invested in systems, not relationships.
- People-based anger (betrayal, disrespect) = identity invested in social bonds.
- "I don't really get angry" = most suppressed emotion → highest diagnostic value. Whatever DOES break through IS their real boundary.

## Layer 6: Необратимость

**Core question:** What do they consider truly irreversible?

**How to probe:**
- Ask: "Is there a decision you'd undo if you could?"
- If "no" → ask: "What about something that happened TO you that can't be undone?"
- Listen for whether they reframe irreversibility itself ("nothing is truly irreversible with enough time/technology").

**What it reveals:**
- Reframing irreversibility = a defense against some specific irreversible loss the subject is bracing against (a death, a severed relationship, a missed window). Find what they're actually defending against — don't presuppose which one.
- Accepting irreversibility = mature relationship with loss OR low attachment.
- The specific thing they name as irreversible = their deepest attachment.

# Layers 7–13 (v3.2 — adapted from clinical-axis + psychometric instruments, as MEASUREMENT not therapy)

Same rules as 1–6: a conversation, never a questionnaire. Where a layer has an academic typology (attachment type, attribution axes, mode-under-load), hold it as a **silent profiler yardstick** scored from speech — like the 14 emotion families in `granularity_instrument.md`, never read the labels to the subject. Each probes a real causal/diagnostic angle the 7 domains + Layers 1–6 don't reach. Diagnose, don't intervene (Principle 8).

## Layer 7: Стиль привязанности (механика близости + генезис)

**Core question:** When someone close goes quiet / pulls away — what fires FIRST: contact-restoration (anxiety, monitoring their behavior), an internal exit into work, or steady contact with no spike?

**How to probe:**
- Don't ask "what's your attachment style" and never name a type to the subject.
- Take a CONCRETE relationship already surfaced in Phase 1 (partner, friend, family). "When [X] suddenly goes distant or silent — what do you notice in yourself in the first minutes?"
- Listen for the axis: hypervigilance to their behavior (anxious branch) vs fast internal exit into work (avoidant branch) vs steady contact (secure).
- Genesis only if THEY bring it up. Don't propose a childhood origin, don't hint at one: an origin goes into the profile only as their own quote, otherwise `origin: not given`.
- Silent yardstick: anxious / avoidant / secure / disorganized. Scope = general closeness style; the systems-vs-intimacy switch belongs to Layer 10, don't re-derive it here.

**What it reveals:**
- Attachment type as a causal behavior pattern in closeness and under stress — distinct from the phenomenology of loneliness (L4).
- Two branches can co-occur — an anxious root (an inconsistent caregiver) plus an avoidant defense layered on top → disorganized candidate.
- Output goes into Section V (Отношения), not a parallel section.

## Layer 8: Атрибутивный стиль (грамматика объяснения провала/успеха)

**Core question:** When something breaks, where does the cause fly first — outward (circumstances, people, market) or inward (I missed it, I overestimated)? And symmetrically — when it works, whose credit is it?

**How to probe:**
- Not a locus-of-control scale, don't call it "locus."
- Take a real recent failure from Phase 1 and listen to the GRAMMAR of the explanation: "they rejected it / they didn't get it" (external) vs "I framed it wrong / I overestimated" (internal).
- Mirror it onto a success: "is that the system being set up well, or you being good at it?"
- Third check: "one-off slip, or a pattern across your life?" (stable vs unstable, global vs specific).
- Three axes (internal/external × stable/unstable × global/specific) are an internal scoring frame — render flat in output, never as jargon.

**What it reveals:**
- The causal grammar of explaining the past — orthogonal to L2 (forward motivation) and L3 (loss conversion).
- Asymmetry is the signal: e.g. one category attributed internally while everything else gets externally neutralized. That asymmetry predicts whether they'll change behavior or find an external cause. Feeds Section VI + the predictive model (XIII).

## Layer 9: Руминация vs рефлексия (что мысль делает с проблемой)

**Core question:** When a thought keeps returning to a problem — does it loop in place with no exit (rumination), or does each pass add an angle and move toward resolution (reflection)?

**How to probe:**
- Don't ask "do you overthink." Take an unresolved situation: "when it comes up on its own — does it spin the same way each time, or turn a new side?"
- Differentiator: rumination = one formulation on repeat, tied to the past/an error, doesn't move; reflection = adds an angle, generates action.
- Cross-check for an off-switch — and watch for an architectural defense AGAINST ruminative overload presented as a choice (e.g. "one focus per day so I don't overload my head"); log it as a symptom, don't prescribe.
- Flat register to the subject: "loops vs turns a new side." Strictly diagnostic — NOT "stop ruminating" (that's the CBT line, Principle 8).

**What it reveals:**
- A mechanism distinct from anxiety (anxiety = future, rumination = past/errors) and from cognitive-style-in-norm (Section II measures scanning/deep-dive, not loop-stickiness).
- Resolves a "can't switch the mind off / thinking all the time" report when it surfaces: is that a reflective stream (resource) or a ruminative capture (cost) masked as a deliberate choice? Feeds Sections II + III.

## Layer 10: Граница под давлением (self-erasure vs system-integrity, switch-point)

**Core question:** When someone's need presses on yours, where does the boundary break — do you yield to keep the bond (erase yourself), or hold hard and get angry at the protocol violation (system integrity)? And WHEN did one mode switch to the other?

**How to probe:**
- Not the quiz item "do you often sacrifice your needs," don't say "people-pleasing" to the subject.
- Take one concrete episode of yielding AND one of refusing from Phase 1. Contrast: where they erased a need for acceptance vs where they held a boundary to the death.
- Probe straight into the seam, using whatever family role actually surfaced in Phase 1 (mediator, fixer, invisible one, the one who carried someone else's state): "Back when you held that role at home — was there an option to say no? When did it appear?" If no such role surfaced, ask it of the concrete yielding episode instead — don't presuppose a caregiving/absorber role.
- Narrow scope to the systems-vs-intimacy SWITCH so you don't re-derive Layer 7's general anxious-self-erasure root.
- Output = a map of where the boundary is/isn't and why, NOT "learn to say no" (aspiration/therapy).

**What it reveals:**
- Mechanically different from L5/L4: anger = boundary FIRED, people-pleasing = boundary did NOT fire.
- Catches a split boundary — e.g. hard in one arena (systems, work, principles) yet historically absent in another (intimacy, family) — when the subject shows one. The switch-point between the two modes is a causal output neither L5 (boundary fired) nor L4 (loneliness phenomenon) produces. Separates two roots: anxious-attachment self-erasure vs a learned caretaker/role-imposed self-erasure.

## Layer 11: Временна́я перспектива (из какого времени принимает решения)

**Core question:** Which time does the person actually decide from — pulled back (nostalgia/regret), stuck in the present (hedonism/fatalism), or living in the future — and is the future a PLAN (agency) or a DEFERRAL (avoiding the present)?

**How to probe:**
- Not the Zimbardo inventory, don't name "time perspective."
- Listen to temporal grammar across the whole session: which tense they speak in more readily and densely. "What takes up more room in your head — what was, what is, or what will be?"
- The key load-bearing differentiator (else it overlaps plans.md): future-as-plan (agency) vs future-as-deferral (avoiding the present) — make that the carrying output.
- Watch for ACTIVE past-severance (the subject treats an earlier self as a closed chapter, real but disowned) — that's data, often paired with a vivid-but-narrow accessible window.
- Diagnostic: "describes the wiring," never "live in the moment / plan more."

**What it reveals:**
- Dominant time horizon as a causal decision pattern — plans.md OPERATIONALIZES the future (A→G) but never diagnoses the orientation itself.
- Rare combinations surface here — a person can hold two horizons at once that don't normally co-occur (e.g. a deferral-heavy future alongside a vividly mourned past, or present-fatalism alongside a detailed long-range plan). Note the combination; don't expect a fixed signature. Severance often ← amnesia of a painful period (defense); a long horizon often ← early safety. Feeds Section I + plans.md.

## Layer 12: Сигнатурные силы vs выученные навыки (заряжает vs истощает)

**Core question:** What does the person do easily and WITH LIFT (signature strength) vs what they're good at but it DRAINS them (learned skill)?

**How to probe:**
- Not the VIA-240, no "signature strengths / virtues" vocabulary.
- Probe the ENERGY trace, not quality: "after which work do you come out charged, and after which wrung out — even if you did it well?"
- Probe the seam where a paid/important strip is minimized: "X is what pays, yet you've squeezed it down to the bare minimum — is that efficiency, or does it quietly drain you?"
- Cross with Дело: a domain can score high yet be built on draining skills.
- Diagnostic: a map of where strength/skill is and why a strip is abandoned — NEVER "develop your strengths" (aspiration; would also nudge a deliberately-parked strip, against a deliberately-parked priority).

**What it reveals:**
- Separates energizing strengths from draining skills — causal to burnout and the "is this person in the right role" check that Дело (WHAT they build + operational power) doesn't make.
- Often explains why a profitable strip is parked while energy pours into unpaid work — a puzzle the domain scores alone leave unsolved.

## Layer 13: Когнитивный режим под нагрузкой + точки слома (lead: режим)

**Core question:** Does thinking stay analytical under stress/overload or switch to another mode (lead axis) — and where does it predictably go rigid (secondary axis)?

**How to probe:**
- SPLIT the bundle; lead with the LOW-RISK half — mode-under-load. "When you're overloaded or in a crisis — does your thinking stay this analytical, or switch?" (separate axis from granularity).
- Secondary half (break-points) is strictly diagnosis-only: listen for absolutes in natural speech on charged topics and distinguish a calibrated analytical position from rigid black-and-white under a trigger.
- NOT a CBT distortion checklist to the subject, NOT "catch your distortion," NOT restructuring thought — that's exactly the therapy engine this instrument refuses (Principle 8). Strip distortion vocabulary from output; describe as flat mechanics. The strictest register-watch in the set.

**What it reveals:**
- Section II catches scanning/deep-dive/burst in NORM; this catches WHERE it breaks systematically and WHETHER the mode changes under load — a distinct predictor of crisis behavior.
- Many absolute formulations need disambiguating: analytical precision (calibrated) vs rigid black-and-white under specific triggers; and whether the analyst holds under overload or flips. Feeds Section II + the predictive model (XIII, crisis behavior).
