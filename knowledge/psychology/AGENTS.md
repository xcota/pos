# psychology/

Psychology and self-knowledge: profiling methods, frameworks for understanding people (including yourself), emotional patterns, and the mental models you use to interpret behavior. Privacy note: personal profiles are sensitive — keep them out of anything you share.

## What goes here

- Profiling frameworks and methodologies
- Concepts for understanding emotion, motivation, behavior
- Patterns you've noticed in yourself or others
- Notes distilled from psychology courses/books (the source course can live in `knowledge/courses/`)

## How entities link

- Frameworks link to the [[course]] / [[research]] they came from.
- Profile nodes (if you keep them) link to the [[person]] they describe.
- Patterns link to the concepts that explain them.

## Discipline

- Demystify: ground everything in physiology, behavior, and evidence — no esoteric framing.
- Separate observation from interpretation (see `knowledge/conflicts/AGENTS.md`).
- Report patterns, don't pathologize.

## When to read

- When profiling someone or preparing for an interaction.
- When you need a framework to make sense of a behavioral pattern.
