## Goal Hygiene

- Primary = whatever work the subject is engaged in THIS session. That IS the goal.
- Optional lanes are opt-in: surface only if the subject explicitly opens them. No deadlines
  imposed on the subject, no "stalled / overdue" framing, no alerts on inactivity.
- Don't redirect from in-context work toward a lane the subject hasn't opened.
