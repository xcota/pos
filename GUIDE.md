# 📖 Guide — using your Personal OS

Everything you need in one page. No jargon.

![How it works](docs/how-to.svg)

---

## What this is

An **AI that remembers you.** It lives as plain files in one folder. You talk to it inside
[Claude Code](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code); it remembers across every session,
does things for you, reflects your patterns back, and gets to know you over time. Everything stays
on your machine.

---

## Start (once)

1. Open this folder in Claude Code.
2. Type **`start`**.

That's it. `start` chats with you for a bit, then builds your personal space. You never type a
setup command — it does everything for you. Stop anytime; type `start` again to pick up where you left off.

---

## Using it, day to day

You mostly just **talk to it**. When you want it to *remember* or *find* something, use one of
these four words:

| Say… | and it will… |
|------|--------------|
| **anything** | answer, or do the task — it already knows your context |
| **`save`** | remember what just happened (writes it to your memory) |
| **`recall <thing>`** | find a past note by *meaning*, not just keywords |
| **`dream`** | tidy up its memory and connect the dots (do this now and then) |

Drop notes, links, and thoughts into the **`daily/`** folder (or just tell it) whenever you want.
That's the raw material it learns from.

---

## Where your stuff lives (you rarely need this)

| Folder | What's in it |
|--------|--------------|
| `context/` | who you are, your goals, your operating rules |
| `knowledge/` | your notes, linked together like a mini-Wikipedia (`[[like this]]`) |
| `daily/` | your day-by-day notes |
| `state/` | what you're currently working on, saved between sessions |

Your writing is **yours** — plain Markdown you can read, edit, back up, or move anywhere.

---

## Good to know

- **It's honest.** It won't claim it did something it didn't. If it's unsure, it says so.
- **It works offline.** The optional "find by meaning" feature downloads one small model the first
  time, then never needs the internet.
- **It's private.** Nothing leaves your machine. No cloud, no accounts, no tracking.
- **Curious how the brain works?** Ask it to explain [`METHODOLOGY.md`](METHODOLOGY.md) — the ten
  ideas that make it tick.
